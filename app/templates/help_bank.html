Connecting your bank

{{ info.business_name }} reads your accounts through Akahu, a New Zealand open finance service that works with all the main banks. Access is read only: nothing can move money.

Connect your bank

{% if info.connect %}
  1. In {{ info.business_name }}, go to Settings → Bank connection and choose Connect your bank. Only household admins can do this.
  2. You’re taken to Akahu. Sign in, or create a free Akahu account. The first time, Akahu asks you to confirm your identity with your NZ driver licence or passport and to set up two-step sign-in.
  3. Choose your bank and sign in to it inside Akahu. Your banking password goes to your bank, never to {{ info.business_name }}.
  4. Tick the accounts to share. Leave out anything you don’t want in your budget; you can change this later at my.akahu.nz.
  5. Approve. You’re brought back to {{ info.business_name }} and the first sync starts. It takes a minute or two.
{% else %}

Bank connections for new households aren’t open yet. You can set up your household, bills and categories now; we’ll email you when connecting is available.

{% endif %}

After connecting

  • Choose what counts. In Settings → Accounts, tick Balance counts for accounts whose money you spend day to day, and Budget counts for accounts whose spending and income belong in your budget. Savings, KiwiSaver and loans are usually left out.
  • Add your bills. Bills → Add, or turn a past payment into a bill from its menu. Payments are matched automatically from then on.
  • Daily sync. {{ info.business_name }} checks for new transactions once a day (Settings → Pay cycle sets the time). Admins can press Sync now on the Overview at any time.
  • Banks take a while. New card purchases show as pending first and settle a day or two later; some banks only send transactions overnight.

Disconnect

Settings → Bank connection → Disconnect stops {{ info.business_name }} reading your bank and tells Akahu to end the connection. The transactions already synced stay until you delete them (or your account). You can also remove access at my.akahu.nz.

Your own household: an Akahu personal app

Only you see this section, as site owner. A personal app connects your own Akahu account and nobody else’s.

  1. Sign up at my.akahu.nz, confirm your identity and turn on two-step sign-in.
  2. Connect your bank inside Akahu and choose the accounts to share.
  3. Open Developers in my.akahu.nz and create a personal app.
  4. Copy the App ID Token (starts app_token_) and the User Access Token (starts user_token_).
  5. In {{ info.business_name }}: Settings → Bank connection → paste both under Personal app tokens, then Save and test.

Opening Connect to subscribers

Needed before other households can connect their banks.

  1. Contact Akahu (developers.akahu.nz) about a Full App and complete their app accreditation. Ask whether you also need accreditation under the Customer and Product Data Act.
  2. In the app’s settings, add this redirect address exactly: {{ callback }}
  3. Put the app’s details in stack.env: AKAHU_CLIENT_ID=app_token_… and AKAHU_CLIENT_SECRET=…, then restart {{ info.business_name }}.
  4. Test with your own household: Settings → Bank connection → Connect your bank.
  5. Then switch sign-up to open or approval in Site details.
