Security

How {{ info.business_name }} protects your information.

Signing in

  • Every sign-in needs your password and a code from an authenticator app. One-time backup codes cover a lost phone.
  • Passwords are stored scrambled with scrypt, never as written. Common passwords are refused.
  • Repeated wrong passwords or codes pause sign-in. Sign-ins end after 30 days, or a week without use, and you can sign out every device at once.

Your bank connection

  • Bank access is read only, through Akahu. {{ info.business_name }} never sees your internet banking password.
  • Connection tokens are encrypted and never shown again once saved.
  • You can disconnect at any time, here or at my.akahu.nz.

Your data

  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Each household’s data is stored separately, and every request checks you belong to the household you’re viewing.
  • Read-only members can’t change anything; this is enforced on the server.
  • Backups you download can be locked with a passphrase. They never include bank connection tokens.
  • Pages are protected against other websites embedding them or sending changes on your behalf.

Reporting a security problem

If you think you’ve found a security problem, please email {% if info.security_email %}{{ info.security_email }}{% else %}us (see Contact){% endif %} with what you found and how to reproduce it. Please don’t access other people’s data or disrupt the service while testing. We’ll reply within 5 working days and keep you updated while we fix it.
