Privacy policy

{% if info.policy_date %}Last updated {{ info.policy_date }}.{% endif %} This policy explains how {{ who }} (“we”) collects, uses and protects personal information in {{ info.business_name }}, under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are

{{ who }}{% if info.nzbn %} (NZBN {{ info.nzbn }}){% endif %}{% if info.address %}, {{ info.address }}{% endif %}. Our privacy officer is {{ info.privacy_officer or 'the person named on our contact page' }}{% if info.privacy_email %}, {{ info.privacy_email }}{% endif %}.

What we collect

  • Your account: username, email address, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, the key for your two-step sign-in codes (encrypted), and your display settings.
  • Your household: its name and who belongs to it, with their roles.
  • Bank information, only once you connect a bank through Akahu: account names and numbers as your bank shows them, balances, transactions (date, amount, description, merchant), pending transactions and, for loans, interest rate and repayment details.
  • What you add: categories, rules, bills, notes, tags, mortgage history and property values.
  • Security records: failed sign-in attempts and the network address they came from, kept briefly to stop password guessing.

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, analytics services or social media pixels.

Why we collect it

To provide {{ info.business_name }}: sign you in securely, show your balances, bills and spending, work out your budget, and send you emails you need (confirming your address, password resets, account approval). We don’t sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Where it comes from

From you, from people in your household who invite you, and from your bank through Akahu when you choose to connect. Akahu only shares the accounts you approve, and you can withdraw that approval at any time in {{ info.business_name }} (Settings, Bank connection) or at my.akahu.nz.

Who can see it

  • People you add to your household, with the access you give them.
  • Our service providers, only so they can run the service: {% if info.hosting_provider %}{{ info.hosting_provider }} (hosting), {% endif %}{% if info.email_provider %}{{ info.email_provider }} (email), {% endif %}and Akahu (bank connection). They must protect it and may not use it for anything else.
  • Anyone the law requires us to tell, such as a court order.

The person who runs this site can manage sign-ins and households, but can only see a household’s money if they’re a member of it.

Where it’s stored

Your information is stored in {{ info.data_location or 'New Zealand' }}{% if info.hosting_provider %} with {{ info.hosting_provider }}{% endif %}. If that is outside New Zealand, we only use providers subject to privacy protections comparable to the Privacy Act, as required by Information Privacy Principle 12.

How we protect it

Encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypted bank connection tokens and two-step keys, two-step sign-in for everyone, separate storage for each household, and limits on sign-in attempts. See Security for more.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. When you delete your account (Settings, You), your login is removed straight away, along with any household where you were the only member. Copies in our backups are overwritten within {{ info.backup_days or '30' }} days. Records of failed sign-ins are kept for no more than an hour.

Your rights

You can see and download your household’s information at any time (Settings, Backup and export), and correct it in the app. You can ask us for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, or to correct it, by emailing {% if info.privacy_email %}{{ info.privacy_email }}{% else %}our privacy officer{% endif %}. We’ll respond within 20 working days.

If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled a privacy matter, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

If something goes wrong

If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we’ll tell the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as practicable, as the Privacy Act requires.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to run the service: keeping you signed in, the step between your password and code, the household you’re viewing, and a short-lived check while you connect your bank. None track you across other sites.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
