Getting started

  1. Create your household and confirm your email address. Then scan the code with an authenticator app and save your backup codes.
  2. Set your payday in Settings → Pay cycle.
  3. Connect your bank: see Connecting your bank.
  4. Choose what counts in Settings → Accounts.
  5. Add your bills. They’re ticked off as they’re paid.
  6. Invite your household from Settings → People.

The numbers

  • A day until payday: this pay’s income, less every bill due before your next payday, less what you’ve spent so far, divided by the days left.
  • Bills to come: bills due before payday with no matching payment yet.
  • Transfers between your own accounts aren’t counted as spending or income.
